Privacy Policy: Retail Book

Customer app · Package name com.madisoftworks.retailbook.customer

Developer: MADI Softworks, Sargodha, Punjab, Pakistan

Effective date: 15 November 2026

1. About this policy

Retail Book ("the app") lets you join the shops you buy from, see the credit (udhaar) record each shop keeps for you, browse the mobile packages a shop offers, and send requests for packages, EasyLoad and credit. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, where it is stored and the choices you have.

Retail Book is a record-keeping app. No money is paid, lent or transferred through the app. Payments between you and a shop happen outside the app (for example in cash, JazzCash or Easypaisa), and the app only records them.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

InformationWhy it is needed
Name, email address and password (or your Google account name and email if you use "Continue with Google") To create your account and sign you in. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us or to shops.
Phone numberSo the shop can recognise you, and as the default number for package and EasyLoad requests.
Owner code of a shop you joinTo find the shop owner and link your account to their shop.
Package requests: mobile number, network, package name and priceSo the shop can activate the package you asked for.
EasyLoad requests: network, mobile number and amountSo the shop can send the mobile balance you asked for.
Credit and repayment requests: amount, and the account name and number you want money sent toSo the shop can review the request and record it in your ledger.

Information shops record about you

A shop you join keeps your ledger: entries such as "you got on credit" or "you paid", amounts, notes and dates, plus the outcome of your requests (for example a reason when a request is declined). The app shows these records to you. The shop owner controls these records; see section 6.

Information stored only on your phone

  • A copy of your shops, ledger and requests, so the app works offline and syncs when you are back online.
  • Your language choice and which notifications you have already seen.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, call logs, SMS messages or browsing history. It does not use analytics or advertising trackers and does not show ads. If this changes, this policy will be updated first.

3. How we use your information

  • To run your account and keep you signed in.
  • To link you with the shops you join and show the records they keep for you.
  • To deliver your requests to the shop and show you their status.
  • To show notifications on your phone when a request is approved, declined, or an EasyLoad is sent.
  • To keep the service secure, for example by signing you out if your account is deleted or disabled.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Who can see your information

  • Shops you join. Only the owner of a shop you joined (or that added you) can see your name, phone number, email, ledger and the requests you sent to that shop. Other shops and other customers cannot see them.
  • Service providers. The app uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) to store and sync data, and Google Sign-In if you choose it. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy terms: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Legal reasons. We may share information if the law requires it.

5. Where your data is stored and how it is protected

  • Data is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database servers in the Asia-Southeast region (Singapore).
  • All data sent between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Database security rules make sure each customer can only read their own records and each shop owner only their own shop.
  • The copy kept on your phone stays in the app's private storage and is deleted when you log out or uninstall the app.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your account information is kept while your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time:

  • Email ammadyounas.tech@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my Retail Book account".
  • We will delete your sign-in account, profile, phone number, shop links and the requests you sent within 30 days, and confirm by email.

The ledger a shop keeps about you belongs to that shop's business records. After your account is deleted it is no longer linked to you in the app, but the shop owner may keep their own records. You can ask the shop owner directly to remove them.

Logging out or uninstalling removes the copy stored on your phone but does not delete your account.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See your information in the app at any time.
  • Change your phone number when joining a shop or sending a request.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it (see section 6).

8. Children

Retail Book is meant for adults (18+). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example if ads are added. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
